## About

My day job is IT support and systems administration: networks, VLANs, Wi-Fi dead zones and the occasional printer that has chosen violence. Once the tickets are closed I switch sides and build software: full-stack apps, AI-driven automations and the tooling around them.

Three rules I work by:

- **Local-first.** Your data should live on your machine, not in someone else's cloud.
- **Automate, then understand.** If a task repeats, it gets a workflow. If it breaks, I want to know why.
- **Six hours to automate a five-minute task is a fair trade.** I will not be taking questions.

Next stop: cybersecurity. I'm training on Hack The Box and Cybrary, learning to break things on purpose instead of by accident.

## Featured projects

### 💸 [appFinanzas](https://github.com/RODknight22/appFinanzas)

A personal finance app that runs entirely on your computer, built for people who earn in one currency and spend in another.

- Multi-currency accounts with historical exchange rates and real transfer costs
- Bank statement import (CSV / Excel) with automatic categorization and no duplicates

### 📰 [n8n-daily-briefing-ai](https://github.com/RODknight22/n8n-daily-briefing-ai)

An automated morning newsletter covering cybersecurity, AI and finance.

- Pulls 6 RSS feeds in parallel and has Google Gemini compile a structured HTML briefing
- Delivers over SMTP, so it works on a home lab with no OAuth callbacks
- Sends an alert email if any step fails; runs on Gemini's free tier at zero cost

`n8n` `Google Gemini` `SMTP` `RSS`

## Sobre mí

Mi trabajo es soporte IT y administración de sistemas: redes, VLANs, zonas sin Wi-Fi y alguna impresora que ha elegido la violencia. Cuando cierro los tickets cambio de bando y desarrollo software: aplicaciones full-stack, automatizaciones con IA y las herramientas que las rodean.

Tres reglas con las que trabajo:

- **Local primero.** Tus datos deben vivir en tu equipo, no en la nube de otro.
- **Automatizar y después entender.** Si una tarea se repite, se convierte en un flujo. Si falla, quiero saber por qué.
- **Seis horas para automatizar una tarea de cinco minutos es un trato justo.** No acepto preguntas.

Siguiente parada: ciberseguridad. Entreno en Hack The Box y Cybrary, aprendiendo a romper cosas a propósito y no por accidente.
